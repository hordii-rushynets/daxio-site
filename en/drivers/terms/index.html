Driver Partner Terms

Draft. Requires review by a lawyer before publication.

Effective from: "[effective date]" · Revision 1

1. General

  • These terms are a public offer by "[Name of sole proprietor/LLC]", code "[EDRPOU/RNOKPP]" ("Daxio") to persons who want to perform rides through the "Daxio" app ("Driver"), under arts. 633, 641, 642 of the Civil Code of Ukraine. Acceptance is submitting a driver application with consent to these terms.
  • The Driver acts independently as a partner, not as a Daxio employee. The relationship is not an employment one.
  • The Driver may work in a rented Daxio electric car (under a separate rental agreement) or in their own car.

2. Driver requirements

  • Age 23 or older; at least 2 years of driving experience; a valid driving licence of the right category.
  • Passport or ID card and a photo of the driving licence; consent to personal data processing.
  • An own car must be roadworthy, have valid compulsory third-party liability insurance (OSCPV) and meet Daxio's condition and appearance requirements.
  • The Driver is responsible for the accuracy of data and must promptly report changes (loss of the right to drive, change of car, etc.).

3. Application and admission

  • The application is submitted in the driver app. A Daxio manager reviews it and may approve or reject it. Daxio need not explain a rejection except where the law requires.
  • For drivers without their own car the manager assigns an electric car from the Daxio fleet and offers a rental agreement.

4. How work operates

  • The Driver chooses when to go online and which orders to accept. After accepting an order the Driver must complete it properly, without unjustified cancellations.
  • The Driver follows traffic rules, tariffs and safety rules, is polite and tidy, does not smoke in the car and carries no more passengers than seats.
  • The Driver carries children, animals and luggage in line with the extras ordered and uses child restraints when needed.
  • The Driver does not agree prices outside the app and does not offer riders trips bypassing the service.

5. Commission and settlements

  • Driver in a Daxio electric car: 0% commission on rides; the Driver pays weekly rent and a deposit under the rental agreement.
  • Driver in their own car: Daxio commission is 10% of the ride fare (excluding tips). The commission may change with 14 days' notice.
  • The ride fare follows the Daxio tariff. Cash goes directly to the Driver; card payments (monobank) are credited to the Driver's in-app balance net of commission (if any) and other amounts due to Daxio.
  • Tips belong to the Driver in full. Waiting fees and extras are part of the ride fare.
  • Payouts follow the procedure and timing shown in the app. The Driver pays their own taxes and charges on income.
  • Daxio may set off the Driver's debts (rent, contractual fines, commission on cash rides) against earnings.

6. Ratings, bonuses and quality

  • Riders rate the Driver after rides. Rating, completion and cancellation rates and complaints affect access to orders and bonuses.
  • Bonus programs (for ride counts, activity at certain hours, etc.) follow the terms published in the app and on the website. Daxio may change or end bonus programs without cancelling bonuses already earned.
  • For a low rating or violations Daxio may warn, temporarily restrict access or block the account.

7. Prohibited conduct and blocking

  • Prohibited: handing the account to another person; providing false data; driving under the influence; charging riders beyond the tariff; rudeness, discrimination, harassment; route or payment fraud; GPS spoofing; deliberate damage to Daxio property.
  • Daxio may temporarily or permanently block an account for breach of these terms, serious complaints, loss of driving rights or false documents. In a dispute the Driver may contact support; Daxio responds within 10 business days.
  • Blocking does not release the Driver from rental obligations or from settlements for completed rides.

8. Liability

  • The Driver is liable for passenger safety, damage caused by their actions, traffic fines and other administrative offences they commit.
  • Daxio is responsible for the platform and timely settlements. Daxio is not liable for failures caused by mobile networks, force majeure or third parties.
  • Liability for damage to a rented car is set by the rental agreement.

9. Personal data

  • Daxio processes the Driver's data under the Privacy Policy and the Consent to Personal Data Processing.

10. Term, changes, termination

  • The terms are open-ended. The Driver may stop at any time after fulfilling obligations (settlements, returning the car). Daxio may end cooperation with 7 days' notice, or immediately for material breaches.
  • Daxio may change the terms by publishing a new revision at least 7 days before it takes effect.
  • Disputes are settled by negotiation and then by court under Ukrainian law.
