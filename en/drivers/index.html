Driver FAQ

Who can drive with Daxio?
Drivers aged 23+ with a category B licence and at least 2 years of driving experience. You need to pass a document check and accept the driver terms.
Which documents do I need?
A passport or ID card and your driving licence (JPG, PNG or PDF up to 10 MB). For your own car — vehicle details and valid motor liability insurance.
How do I apply?
Install the driver app, sign in with your phone number, choose a Daxio rental car or your own car, fill in the form and upload documents.
How long is the review?
Usually 1–2 business days. The status is shown in the app; if rejected you'll see why and can apply again.
What is the commission?
0% in a Daxio rental car — you only pay a fixed weekly rent (see the Driver terms). In your own car — 12% of the fare, with no payout fees. Tips are never charged commission.
How does renting work?
A fixed weekly fee plus a one-time deposit before you start. Prices for each model are on the drivers page. Invoices arrive in the app and you can pay by card via monobank.
What if I pay rent late?
The app reminds you before the due date. If payment is overdue, access to orders may be paused until it's paid.
Can I choose my own hours?
Yes. You decide when to go online and how long to work.
How do I get paid?
You keep cash fares immediately. Card fares, tips, compensations and bonuses show in the app and are paid out on the schedule in your agreement.
Do I need to be a sole proprietor (FOP)?
Yes, drivers work as sole proprietors (FOP) and pay their own taxes. You can register online in Diia; contact support if you have questions.
Am I paid for waiting and cancellations?
Yes. Paid waiting after the free minutes and late-cancellation fees from riders go to you.
How is my rating calculated?
It's the average rider score for your trips. A high rating and acceptance rate affect your bonuses.
Why could my account be blocked?
For a consistently low rating, unsafe driving, rudeness, fraud, driving a car not in your profile, or letting someone else use your account.
What are the requirements for my own car?
A 4–5-door passenger car in good working order with a clean interior, working seatbelts and valid motor liability insurance.
Who pays fines and fuel or charging?
Traffic fines are the driver's. In your own car, fuel or charging is on you; for Daxio cars the terms are in the rental agreement.
How do bonuses work?
There's a welcome bonus, weekly quests, peak-hour boosts, a rating bonus, daily streaks and referrals. Progress is shown in the app.
How do I contact support?
Use the site form, the driver app, or the details on the Contacts page. In an accident or emergency, call 112 first.
