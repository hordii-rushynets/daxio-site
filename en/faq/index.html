FAQ

For riders

How do I book a ride?
Sign in with your phone number, enter pickup and destination, choose a car class and extras, then confirm. The app shows the driver, car model, plate and arrival time.
Can the price change after I book?
The price is locked when you book. It only changes if you change the route, add a stop, or keep the driver waiting past the free time.
Why does the price vary?
The fare depends on distance, trip time, car class and demand. At peak times it can be higher, but you always see it before confirming.
How long does the driver wait for free?
3 minutes after arrival. After that it is 3 UAH per started minute, capped at 60 UAH per ride.
Can I cancel for free?
Yes, within 2 minutes after a driver accepts. Cancelling later, or once the driver has arrived, costs 40 UAH, which compensates the driver for the trip to you.
How can I pay?
By card via monobank or cash to the driver. You choose the payment method before booking.
Why is money on hold on my card?
When you book, the amount is only reserved. After the ride the actual fare is charged and the rest is released automatically; if the ride is cancelled the whole hold is released. Release time depends on your bank.
Can I add a stop?
Yes, up to 2 stops on the way, 20 UAH each. Add them when you book.
Can I book in advance?
Yes. Schedule a ride at least 30 minutes and up to 7 days ahead. We start looking for a driver in advance.
How do I ride with a child?
Choose the Child seat option and a driver with a seat will come. It costs 50 UAH extra. Traffic rules require small children to ride in a proper car seat or booster.
Can I bring a pet?
Yes, choose the Pet option (40 UAH extra) and only drivers who accept pets will get the order. Small pets are best carried in a carrier.
I have large luggage
Add the Luggage option (30 UAH) so the driver knows in advance and has trunk space ready.
What is a Quiet ride?
A free option: the driver won't start conversations, so you can rest or work in peace.
How do I share my trip?
During the ride tap Share ride and your loved ones get a link to follow the car and route live.
What if I feel unsafe?
Tap the SOS 112 button in the app during the ride to call emergency services right away. Then tell us via support and we will look into it.
I left something in the car
Contact support on the site or in the app with the ride date, route and a description of the item. We will contact the driver and help get it back.
How do promo codes work?
Enter the code in the app before booking. The discount is a percentage of the fare up to a set UAH maximum. See each code's terms on the promos page.
How do I rate a driver or complain?
After the ride, rate the driver and tip if you like. If something went wrong, contact support with the ride date and route.
What does raising the price mean?
If no car is found for a while on a cash ride, you can add a few hryvnias to the fare so the order stands out to drivers.

For drivers

Who can drive with Daxio?
Drivers aged 23+ with a category B licence and at least 2 years of driving experience. You need to pass a document check and accept the driver terms.
Which documents do I need?
A passport or ID card and your driving licence (JPG, PNG or PDF up to 10 MB). For your own car — vehicle details and valid motor liability insurance.
How do I apply?
Install the driver app, sign in with your phone number, choose a Daxio rental car or your own car, fill in the form and upload documents.
How long is the review?
Usually 1–2 business days. The status is shown in the app; if rejected you'll see why and can apply again.
What is the commission?
0% in a Daxio rental car — you only pay a fixed weekly rent (see the Driver terms). In your own car — 12% of the fare, with no payout fees. Tips are never charged commission.
How does renting work?
A fixed weekly fee plus a one-time deposit before you start. Prices for each model are on the drivers page. Invoices arrive in the app and you can pay by card via monobank.
What if I pay rent late?
The app reminds you before the due date. If payment is overdue, access to orders may be paused until it's paid.
Can I choose my own hours?
Yes. You decide when to go online and how long to work.
How do I get paid?
You keep cash fares immediately. Card fares, tips, compensations and bonuses show in the app and are paid out on the schedule in your agreement.
Do I need to be a sole proprietor (FOP)?
Yes, drivers work as sole proprietors (FOP) and pay their own taxes. You can register online in Diia; contact support if you have questions.
Am I paid for waiting and cancellations?
Yes. Paid waiting after the free minutes and late-cancellation fees from riders go to you.
How is my rating calculated?
It's the average rider score for your trips. A high rating and acceptance rate affect your bonuses.
Why could my account be blocked?
For a consistently low rating, unsafe driving, rudeness, fraud, driving a car not in your profile, or letting someone else use your account.
What are the requirements for my own car?
A 4–5-door passenger car in good working order with a clean interior, working seatbelts and valid motor liability insurance.
Who pays fines and fuel or charging?
Traffic fines are the driver's. In your own car, fuel or charging is on you; for Daxio cars the terms are in the rental agreement.
How do bonuses work?
There's a welcome bonus, weekly quests, peak-hour boosts, a rating bonus, daily streaks and referrals. Progress is shown in the app.
How do I contact support?
Use the site form, the driver app, or the details on the Contacts page. In an accident or emergency, call 112 first.
