4. Fare and payment

  • The fare is calculated under the tariff in force when ordering and shown before confirmation. The final fare may change if the rider changes the route, adds stops or extras, or the ride takes longer because of waiting.

  • Promo codes follow the rules of the specific promotion, do not combine unless stated, and have no cash value.
